## Signing Process

This CLA is managed via CLA Assistant bot on GitHub. When you submit your first pull request:

1. The CLA Assistant bot will comment on your PR with a link
2. Click the link and sign in with your GitHub account
3. Review this agreement and click "I Agree"
4. The bot will update your PR status to show CLA signed

Your signature applies to all future contributions to VireoStudio. You only need to sign once.
